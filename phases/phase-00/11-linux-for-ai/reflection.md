## Learner reflection

The learner initially missed all three quiz questions, then answered the repeated questions correctly. In a fresh retake, they identified `rsync`'s use for large transfers but missed the meaning of `~` and the Ubuntu package installation sequence.

## Takeaway

`chmod +x` fixes a missing execute bit on a script. It does not fix unrelated permission problems such as access to Docker's daemon socket.
